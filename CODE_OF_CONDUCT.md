# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We want this repository to be a professional, respectful place for people contributing to crane-rental operations software. Participants are expected to communicate constructively, focus discussion on the work, and respect the privacy and operational sensitivity of the project.

## Expected behaviour

Contributors and reviewers should:

- Use professional, inclusive, and respectful language.
- Give feedback on implementation, documentation, and observable behaviour rather than individuals.
- Share only material they are authorised to share, and redact customer data, credentials, personal data, and operationally sensitive records.
- Report security concerns through the private process in the [security policy](.github/SECURITY.md), not through public discussion.
- Respect review decisions, repository guidance, and the role-based access boundaries represented by the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate disclosure of confidential material, credential sharing, misuse of production systems, or disruption of the public service are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns to the repository maintainers through the organisation’s established private support channel. Maintainers may clarify expectations, remove content, limit participation, or take other proportionate action to protect participants and the project.

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, review comments, and other project-related interactions.
