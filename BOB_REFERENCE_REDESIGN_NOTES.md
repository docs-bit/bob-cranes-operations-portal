# BOB Rental Landing Redesign Reference Notes

## Source and scope

The supplied reference is a vertically structured service-business landing page. These notes record verified visual hierarchy patterns for adaptation to BOB Heavy Equipment Rental; they do not copy the source brand, imagery, customer claims, or contact details.

## Tiles 1–2: top-page composition

The reference uses a narrow, white, high-contrast navigation bar floating over a dark photo hero. Its hero layers an uppercase headline, compact service qualifier, two direct actions, and a framed secondary visual card. The lower edge uses an irregular light divider to transition into a pale editorial surface.

Immediately after the hero, a split introduction pairs a compact social/professional proof element with a large purpose statement and single action. A thin editorial line with circular checkpoints crosses the page above four oversized proof metrics. For BOB, this pattern should become a factual operations-readiness narrative and measured service-capability indicators, not fabricated social proof or performance claims.

## Design decisions for BOB

The redesign will apply the compact white navigation, layered dark equipment hero, asymmetric content bands, warm off-white background, orange operational accent, line-led metric rhythm, and direct action hierarchy. BOB’s real crane photography, public rental enquiry form, Sales notification workflow, and portal sign-in will remain integrated.

## Tiles 3–4: services and project proof

The reference groups six services into a tight two-row card grid with iconography, short explanatory copy, and compact directional actions. This is followed by an image-plus-operating-details split module before a horizontally driven project gallery. The gallery uses oversized project imagery, small category tags, and minimal navigation controls. It transitions through an irregular divider into a dark proof/conversion band.

For BOB, this maps to a concise lift-service grid, an operational availability/planning card, and a project-capability gallery built only from supplied BOB equipment and worksite photography. The dark band should present verifiable operational process information and direct enquiry action; it will not invent testimonials, customer ratings, or project claims.

## Tiles 5–6: process and people

The lower-page reference combines a dark lead-generation band with a large, clearly sequenced work-process module. The process uses three staggered horizontal bars in alternating dark, accent, and light surfaces. It then moves into a team-card carousel before the final coverage and conversion area.

For BOB, the corresponding section will use a factual three-step rental pathway: brief and scope, equipment/readiness coordination, and controlled handoff. It will present roles rather than named staff or testimonials, so the page communicates accountability without manufacturing user-generated content or unverified project statistics.

## Tiles 7–8: coverage and closing conversion

The reference closes with a map-like regional coverage composition, a large image-backed estimate call-to-action, and a structured multi-column dark footer. The coverage area uses many named locations and the final CTA repeats the primary action before the footer navigation and contact block.

For BOB, the redesign will use a compact service-readiness coverage list based on the company’s visible operating context, without claiming unsupported coverage. A photo-backed rental-planning call-to-action will route to the existing enquiry form. The footer will preserve the live portal link, primary sections, and operational contact guidance without fabricating telephone, address, social, or working-hour details.
